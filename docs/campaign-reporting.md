# Campaign reporting

The example ledger is fictional. Copy `examples/campaign.csv` outside the repository to maintain your own; never commit private records. Each row represents one successfully sent message, with a unique anonymous `send_id`, ISO `sent_date`, template name and audience segment. Record each outcome as 0 or 1, updating that row when an outcome is observed. Multiple opens or clicks on one message still count once. Choose and consistently apply a conversion definition. Failed or uncertain sends do not belong in the ledger. This manual ledger is separate from the CRM database: copy send metadata and observed outcomes into it deliberately. It does not send mail or collect tracking data.

Install `requirements-reporting.txt` and run the command in the README. Dates are inclusive send-date cohorts, and previous and current periods must not overlap. Outcomes are attributed to the send date, not the date of the event; allow enough time for responses before comparing cohorts. Both periods may have different lengths, so compare rates and sample sizes, not raw counts alone. Empty cohorts have blank rates. The workbook includes counts, rates and 95% Wilson score intervals for every template and segment combination; these intervals describe uncertainty under a binomial model, not proof of a difference. All four rates use sends as the denominator. Missing observations must not be mistaken for known non-events: only use complete, consistently collected data.

Default targets (40% open, 10% click, 15% reply, 5% conversion) are examples, not external benchmarks. Supply fractions between zero and one with `--benchmark-open`, `--benchmark-click`, `--benchmark-reply`, or `--benchmark-conversion`. The slide compares overall cohorts and targets, and reports the largest observed rate change. Audience mix, template mix, time and chance can affect the change; the report does not infer causation. Open tracking can be unreliable due to mail privacy features. No tracking pixel or external service is included.
